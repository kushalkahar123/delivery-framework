## What this call is

Kickoff is where we **convene the team, lock decision-makers, agree on cadence, and re-confirm the engagement framing** that came out of our Calibrate call (Priya, 24 Apr). It is not a working session. It is not a design review. It is the moment we make sure everyone in the room has the same understanding of what we're doing and what we're not doing.

## What came out of Calibrate (read-back)

DC's read of the engagement after the Calibrate call with Priya:

- **Track:** Redesign.
- **Problem statement:** Product Positioning.
- **Client type:** Direction / Story.
- **Redesign+ flag:** Ruled out (no analytics, scope is not surgical).

The work is **brand-coherence-heavy** (≈60%) with **structural support** (≈35%) and **light surface** (≈5%) work. Translation: voice, microcopy, IA-where-positioning-lives, and onboarding will move. The case-study editor, the reading experience, the wordmark, custom domain, and Stripe will not.
